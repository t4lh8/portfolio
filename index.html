About

I'm Talha, a third-year cybersecurity student at Kristiania in Oslo. I finish my bachelor's in 2027 and I'm looking for an internship or a graduate role in security.

My degree covers network and system security, secure design and security monitoring. In lab environments I scan with Nmap, read traffic in Wireshark, test with Kali Linux and go through logs in a SIEM. I use Python to automate tasks and write my own tools.

I'm most interested in SOC work: log analysis, threat detection and incident response. That's why I built ZeroTrust Sentinel, a monitoring platform in C# and ASP.NET Core. It spots unusual logins and traffic, sends live alerts and keeps an audit log.

Next to my studies I teach. I run tech workshops for young people and help kids learn to code. It has taught me to explain technical topics in plain language.

I speak Norwegian, English and Turkish. Outside of tech I train MMA. It keeps me disciplined and calm under pressure.
